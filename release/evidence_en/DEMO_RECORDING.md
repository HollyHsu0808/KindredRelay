## 3. Suggested recording flow (about 2 minutes)

1. In the left sidebar press **Restart the demo**, then press **Add this update** 6 times; the story reaches "she stood up with the walking frame" (u06).
2. Under "Viewing as" choose **Anna** (main caregiver) and press **🎙️ Record what the nurse said**.
3. Press record and read the passage below in English (playing the ward nurse; all of it is fictional), then press stop:

   > She is a little dizzy when she stands up, so the team wants to keep her one more day to be safe. Going home is now planned for Saturday morning.

4. Press **Write out the recording**; Whisper writes out the text. Check it against the recording and correct any mistake directly; tick the lines that have a date, a number or "stop / continue".
5. Fill "Who is speaking?" with **Ward nurse** and press **Add to the timeline**. After Gemma reads the facts, **Going home moved to Sat 24 Oct morning** (orange) appears at the top of the timeline, and the original Friday is struck through.
6. Switch "Viewing as" to **Mia (granddaughter, Melbourne)**: you see the NEW marker, Melbourne time, and on the "Right now" card Going home already changed to Saturday (marked Changed · was Fri 23 Oct).
7. Press **💬 Ask about Elena** and click the small button *When is Elena going home?* (or type it and press **Find out**): the answer lists the nurse's sentence below it (Ward nurse, recording), and the old Friday plan marked earlier.
8. You can also ask *Can she get out of bed on her own?*: the system does not answer yes or no, it only says "only the care team can decide", and lists what the care team said.
9. Ask one more question the records do not cover, e.g. *Will she need a wheelchair at home?*: the screen shows "The updates don't mention this yet."; press **💬 Save it for the doctor**, and a "Questions for the doctor" list appears on the right. Switch to David and ask the same question; the list merges into "asked by Mia, David". Later, when seeing the doctor, press **🎙️ Record the doctor's answers** under the list, and the recording panel lists the questions to ask. If a later update may have answered one of them, the list shows "Maybe answered: …"; after the family confirms, press **✓ That answers it**, or press **✓ Answered** when it was asked at the visit (a wrong press can be undone with Reopen under Answered).
10. You can also press the record button and ask by voice (when Whisper is installed); Whisper fills the question into the input box, and after checking press Find out. Questions about the wound, injections, falls or pain relief get a leaflet attached below the answer (marked as general information, demo content).
