# KindredRelay clinical-safety hazard log

**What this is.** A failure-mode table for the family timeline (the deployed product path) and the parts of the Case Agent that share its guardrails. It is *modelled on* the hazard-log practice of ISO 14971 (risk management for medical devices) and NHS DCB0129 (clinical risk management in the manufacture of health IT): hazard, cause, effect, risk before controls, controls, verification, residual risk, status. **It is not a certified ISO 14971 or DCB0129 safety case**: no clinical safety officer has reviewed it, the severity and likelihood scores are the team's judgement, and the demo uses fictional patients only. KindredRelay is a hackathon prototype, not a medical device.

**How to check it.** Every `path:line` ("quote"), every `test_file.py::test_name` and every `results.json` → `key` = value below is machine-checked by:

```bash
python scripts/check_hazard_log.py        # exit 0 = every citation resolves; the quoted text is on the cited line
```

It fails when a cited line has moved or changed, a cited test no longer exists, a cited result value differs, or a cited test file is neither run by `deploy/validate_abd.py` nor listed in the last section below. Red-team numbers come from the final rerun `release/benchmarks/redteam_final_20261018T024113Z/` (after the DX-05 and PI-04 fixes; deterministic, offline, no model; scope: "deterministic guardrail decisions on fixed adversarial inputs; not model accuracy, not clinical correctness"); metamorphic numbers from `release/benchmarks/metamorphic_final_20261018T024113Z/` (same code). The earlier red-team run `release/benchmarks/redteam_20261017T140024Z/` is kept unchanged as the before-fix record.

**Scales (team judgement, not measured probabilities).** Severity: 1 negligible (confusion, no care impact) · 2 minor (a family member asks again) · 3 moderate (a missed or late care action, recoverable) · 4 major (wrong care action by the family, e.g. a missed appointment or a wrong mobility instruction) · 5 catastrophic (could contribute to serious harm, e.g. a medicine taken that must not be). Likelihood per use of the feature: 1 very unlikely · 2 unlikely · 3 possible · 4 likely · 5 very likely. Score = severity × likelihood (1–25). Residual = the same scores after the listed controls.

**Status.** `open`: a known path to the harm is not closed on the deployed path (or the control is not active). `mitigated`: the known paths are closed and tested; the residual is what the tests cannot rule out. `accepted`: not controlled in code; the residual is accepted for a demonstration with fictional data only and would block any real-world use.

## Not in scope of this log

- The fall-detection model (MediaPipe pose → GRU) is a separate module and is not in the release image (`Dockerfile.release` copies no `handoff_day1/model/`); its hazards (missed fall, false alarm) are not assessed here.
- Data-protection hazards (health data sent to the hosted endpoint) are handled by policy: only fictional cases and public PriMock57 audio were sent (`release/evidence_en/DEV_README_EXCERPT.md:37`); not a clinical hazard in this sense.
- Hardware and the hosted endpoint's availability guarantees are not visible to us.
