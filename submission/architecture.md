# KindredRelay architecture (release build)

Diagram: [`architecture.svg`](architecture.svg) (1920×1080, self-contained, light background).

Reading the diagram: **orange boxes run on AMD Token Factory** (inference via the AMD Token Factory endpoint provided for the AMD Developer Hackathon); green boxes are deterministic code with no model; blue boxes are steps a person does; white boxes run in the browser or on the app server's CPU; dashed boxes are in the repository but not in the deployed app path. The hardware behind the Token Factory endpoint is not visible to the client, so no GPU is named anywhere in this submission.

"Release build" means what `Dockerfile.release` packages: it copies `handoff_day1/agent/`, `care_cases/`, `ui/pages/`, `ui/timeline_storage.py`, `scripts/asr_whisper.py` and `deploy/serve.py` (`Dockerfile.release:35-41`), and `deploy/serve.py:58-59` starts `handoff_day1/ui/pages/shell/kindred_app.py`, whose three pages are Family timeline (default), Evaluation & Safety Scorecard and AMD Runtime (`handoff_day1/ui/pages/shell/kindred_app.py:27-30`).

## Row 1: one update becomes timeline facts

| Box | What it is | Evidence (path:line) |
|---|---|---|
| A person checks the draft | The transcript or photo text is shown for correction; every line with a number, a time, or a stop or continue must be ticked as checked against the recording or photo before "Add to the timeline" is enabled. Lines the caregiver edits are her words, so their facts show as "reported by family, not confirmed". | `handoff_day1/ui/pages/1_Family_timeline.py:991-998` (`tick_boxes`), `:1411` (photo text correction), `:1440`, `:1780` ("Add to the timeline"); `handoff_day1/agent/README.md:29` (`reading.changed_lines` → family authority) |
| Fact extraction: DeepSeek-V4-Flash (AMD Token Factory) | The checked text goes to the text model, which lists the update's facts as JSON; each fact carries a quote copied word for word from the update (at least 3 words). A reply that cannot be read, or that was cut off, yields no facts. | `deploy/release.env.example:2` (`CARERELAY_LLM_MODEL=DeepSeek-V4-Flash`), `handoff_day1/ui/pages/1_Family_timeline.py:889-899` (`llm_extractor(make_client(max_tokens=2000))`), `handoff_day1/agent/episode.py:2120-2168` (prompt text at `:2137-2139`, unreadable → `[]` at `:2165`) |
| Grounding check (no model) | `grounding_problem` rejects a fact unless its quote is in the update word for word (at least 3 words, not right after a negation), its numbers and short field words come from the quote, its date parses and agrees with the words said, and a medicine's status matches its quote. Rejected facts are kept with their reason, not shown as facts. | `handoff_day1/agent/episode.py:262-287` (definition), `:957-960` (called for every proposed fact in `add_update`) |
| Evidence verifier (off in the release) | Optional model-based check of each fact against its source. `KINDRED_VERIFIER_MODE=off` in the release image and env template, so `checker_from_env()` returns `None`. Gate mode is refused unless the verifier card says `validation_operating_point_found` is true; verifier v2 found no valid operating point (0 of 4,048 candidate thresholds), so the gate stays off (fail-closed: it is not switched on unproven). In gate mode a fact that is not `supported_by_model`, or whose date needs review, is withheld. | `Dockerfile.release:14`, `deploy/release.env.example:14`, `handoff_day1/agent/evidence_gate.py:106-114,124-131,150-153`, `deploy/serve.py:42-43`, `handoff_day1/agent/episode.py:969-971`, `release/evidence_en/KAGGLE_VERIFIER_V2_RESULTS.md:3,39-40` |
| Family timeline & handover | A "Right now" card and a newest-first timeline; headlines come from fixed templates, not a model; a family note never overrides a care-team source about the same thing and shows as reported by family; the page states the product does not diagnose or give medical advice. | `handoff_day1/agent/README.md:29` (authority, supersession), `handoff_day1/agent/episode.py:69-76` (`source_authority`), `handoff_day1/agent/timeline_html.py:246` (`plan_headline`), `handoff_day1/ui/pages/1_Family_timeline.py:1934` ("does not diagnose or give medical advice") |

## Row 3: in the repository, outside the deployed app path

| Cross-model semantic check | `scripts/crosscheck_judge.py`: Gemma-4-31B-IT, on the same AMD Token Factory endpoint, acts as a judge layered on the deterministic guardrails. | Other evaluation and verification tools (added 2026-10-17/18) | `scripts/load_tf_endpoint.py` (endpoint load test), `scripts/capacity_model.py` (tokens and busy hours from measured runs), `scripts/analyze_bench_agreement.py` (fact stability), `scripts/metamorphic_eval.py` (metamorphic relations, offline plus an optional live layer), `scripts/primock_gold_eval.py` (PriMock57 external reference), `scripts/verify_claims.py` (recomputes published numbers), `scripts/check_hazard_log.py`, `scripts/check_depth_coverage.py`. ## Known discrepancy to be aware of

- `deploy/release.env.example:4` sets the photo model to `Gemma-4-31B-IT`, but `deploy/run_local.py:32-33` defaults `CARERELAY_PHOTO_MODEL` to `MiniCPM-V-4.7-1B` when nothing is set. The diagram follows the release template. For the demo video, set `CARERELAY_PHOTO_MODEL` explicitly (see `DEMO_VIDEO_SCRIPT_zh-TW.md`).
- `handoff_day1/agent/asr_local.py:3` and `release/evidence_en/DEMO_RECORDING.md:12` say the Docker image has no Whisper; that refers to the older `handoff_day1/Dockerfile` (in the private development repository). The release image (`Dockerfile.release:34`) bakes Whisper base.en in, and `deploy/serve.py:68-73` refuses to start without it unless `CARERELAY_REQUIRE_ASR=0`.
